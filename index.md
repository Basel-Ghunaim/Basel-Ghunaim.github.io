## Background
This project focuses on designing and developing a robotic arm capable of picking and placing different types of objects such as a ball, egg, screw, bolt, and CD. The system combines mechanical gripping with vacuum suction to improve versatility and reliability.

The robotic system integrates multiple hardware and software components, including stepper motors, sensors, a hybrid end effector, and control systems. In addition, the design includes the integration of a camera, artificial intelligence techniques, a Raspberry Pi, and an Arduino to support advanced functionality and system control.

The project aims to demonstrate a complete mechatronic system that combines mechanical design, electronics, control, and intelligent processing within a single integrated platform.

## Results
The project focuses on the design and development of a robotic arm capable of handling multiple types of objects using a hybrid end-effector system. The system integrates stepper motor control, position tracking through step counting and homing, and a vacuum suction mechanism for handling flat objects such as CDs.

Through the work completed so far, the team has developed a good understanding of the available components in the market and how they influence the design choices. This includes evaluation of motors, sensors, and vacuum systems.

The overall system design is still under development and refinement. In addition, the camera-based detection and artificial intelligence components are currently under progress and have not yet been finalized.

The current work has established a solid foundation for further development, integration, and testing of the robotic system.
